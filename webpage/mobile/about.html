About the app

Welcome to AnchorLauncher! We are a team of passionate developers dedicated to creating a game launcher where you can operate all your PC games from one app. Our mission is to provide gamers with a seamless and convenient way to access their game libraries, manage their games, and enhance their gaming experience. With AnchorLauncher, you can easily organize your games, launch them with a single click, and enjoy a personalized gaming experience. We are committed to continuously improving our app and adding new features based on user feedback. We believe in creating a community-driven platform where gamers can share their experiences, suggest new features, and connect with fellow gamers. Thank you for choosing AnchorLauncher as your go-to game launcher!
